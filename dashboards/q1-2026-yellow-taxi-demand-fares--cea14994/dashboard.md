# Q1 2026 Yellow Taxi Performance

Q1 2026 recorded **11.08 million** yellow taxi trips. March was the busiest month (3.95M trips), February the quietest (3.40M), and January landed in between (3.72M). The typical fare stayed steady at a median of $15.60–$16.30, while the typical total amount ranged from $23.05 to $24.00.
