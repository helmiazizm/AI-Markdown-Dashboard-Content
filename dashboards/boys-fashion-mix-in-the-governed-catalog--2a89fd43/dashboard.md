# What Boys' Fashion Looks Like in This Catalog

Boys' fashion in the governed catalog is a **casual, colourful, top-heavy kids' wardrobe** — an assortment signal, not a sales or popularity record. Across the 830 products tagged `gender = 'Boys'`, T-shirts alone account for about 64% of the mix (531 products), and shirts add another 9%. Add shorts and jeans and the top-and-bottom casual apparel story is clear. The colour palette skews bright and primary — blue, white, red, yellow, black, and green. Nearly all Boys items (96%) are casual-use, with only a thin sports (2.5%) and ethnic (1.2%) tail, and the assortment is overwhelmingly Summer (83%).

**Important caveat:** this is a mixed-brand assortment with no price or stock fields, so it measures what the catalog carries, not what boys actually buy. "Nowadays" is also hard to verify here: 646 of 830 Boys rows are collection-year 2012, with only 11 rows in 2013 and 8 watches in 2016. The most recent records point to character/entertainment-branded watches and footwear, but treat that as anecdotal.
